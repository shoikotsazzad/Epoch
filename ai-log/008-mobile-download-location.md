where is the download button at the mobile part?

## Response

On phones, the download button moved into **Settings** to keep the top bar uncluttered:

1. Tap the **Settings** button (the sliders icon, top right).
2. At the top of the sheet, under "How to use", tap **Export**.
3. Choose WAV or MP3 and 1, 3 or 5 minutes, then tap **Render**. Or use **Start recording** to capture what you hear live.

It's two taps away now instead of one. If you'd rather keep a download icon in the phone top bar, I can put it back between Play and Settings; three icons still fit at 390 px.
