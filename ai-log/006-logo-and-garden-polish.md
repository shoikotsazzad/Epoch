- **Dropped:** the four plant symbols on the tips. They turned to noise at 32–64 px, as you predicted.
- **No new dependencies:** the script compiles with the TypeScript already in the project, and the PNGs are rendered with the Chrome installed on this Mac.

**Garden fixes**
- **Start fresh** now takes you back to the very beginning: Night mood, one new bonsai that grows in, and a new seed.
- **Rain** is drawn as bamboo instead of chart bars: thicker at the top, softened ends, a slight bow and darker joints. The removed middles are empty space. Drops fall into small, quick ripples, and the stray circle and ground line are gone.
- **Sunflower** has no teal pond any more. Its seeds are warm cream, grow bigger toward the edge and are slightly oval, with two families of amber spiral arms (every 13th and 21st seed) so the golden spiral shows.
- **Selection ring** is now a faint thin circle instead of dashes.
- **Fewer circles:** each plant has 4 faint rings instead of 8–10, and a brighter ripple travels out only when that plant plays a note.
- **Layout:** plants now keep about 120 px clear of the top bar and the caption/tray (less on short screens), and placement measures edge to edge. Across five random four-plant gardens the closest two circles were 22–36 px apart, with no overlaps.
- **Captions:** one short line, rotating plant by plant every 2 s.
- **Top bar:** ☰ is now a sliders icon with a "Settings" label (icon only on narrower screens), and the panel is titled Settings.
- **Stones:** the triangle lines are barely visible now and show clearly only in X-ray mode.

**Contrast**
- I computed the ratios from the colour tokens, because axe can't read text drawn over the canvas: the subtitle went from 5.37:1 to **7.73:1**, the MELODY/RHYTHM labels from 5.18:1 to **6.83:1**, and captions are 5.37:1.
